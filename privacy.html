My Tax App

Privacy Policy

1. Personal Data Privacy

My Tax App is a personal property income tracking tool. All property records, rental income, and expense receipts are processed privately and securely. Data is never shared, sold, or exposed to third parties.

2. Information Collection & Usage

Information collected (such as rental income entries, expense receipts, and HMRC OAuth authorization tokens) is strictly used for calculating UK property tax obligations and submitting Making Tax Digital (MTD) returns directly to HMRC upon user confirmation.

For privacy questions, contact: mytaxapp.support@gmail.com

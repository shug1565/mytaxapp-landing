HMRC MTD Compliant Privacy Policy & Security Statement

Strict Zero-Human-Sight Policy

1. Privacy & Data Protection Commitment

At My Tax App, user security and privacy are paramount. Your financial records, rental income, receipts, and property details are processed machine-to-machine exclusively in-memory during active sessions.

No administrative staff, developers, or unauthorized humans can view your personal or financial data in plaintext.

2. Information We Collect

To enable Making Tax Digital (MTD) quarterly income tax calculations and HMRC filings, we collect:

  • Account Identifiers: Name and email address.
  • Property & Financial Records: Rental income amounts, allowable expense receipts, property addresses, and mortgage interest details.
  • HMRC Integration Data: Authorization tokens granted directly by HMRC OAuth 2.0.

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is strictly used for:

  • Calculating property tax allowances, Section 24 mortgage relief, and quarterly UK tax obligations.
  • Submitting official quarterly MTD filings directly to HMRC upon your explicit confirmation.
  • Providing automated receipt parsing and income tracking.

4. Third-Party Sharing & HMRC Compliance

We never sell, lease, or monetize your data. Financial data is shared only with HMRC via their secure API when you submit tax returns, under strict UK GDPR controls.
